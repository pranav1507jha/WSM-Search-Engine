Title: Wildcard Queries Permuterm and K-Gram Indexes
Author: Prabhakar Raghavan
Category: Tolerant Retrieval
Year: 2025
Language: English
ACL: public
---
Wildcard queries support prefix, suffix, and infix pattern matching when users are uncertain of exact spelling. The permuterm index appends a terminal dollar symbol to each term and indexes all cyclic rotations so any single wildcard query translates into a prefix range lookup. Alternatively, a bigram or trigram index decomposes terms into overlapping character sequences and intersects candidate terms followed by regex post-filtering.

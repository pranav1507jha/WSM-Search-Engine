Title: Inverted Index Construction and Skip Pointers
Author: Christopher Manning
Category: Indexing
Year: 2024
Language: English
ACL: public
---
An inverted index maps every distinct dictionary term to a sorted postings list of document identifiers and term frequencies. During boolean AND query processing, two postings lists are intersected in linear time using a simultaneous merge walk. Augmenting postings lists with evenly spaced skip pointers of length square root of L allows the intersection algorithm to bypass non-matching document IDs rapidly.

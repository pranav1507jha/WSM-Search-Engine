Title: Phrase Queries Biword and Positional Indexes
Author: Williams and Zobel
Category: Indexing
Year: 2025
Language: English
ACL: public
---
About ten percent of web search queries are phrase queries enclosed in quotation marks, such as stanford university palo alto. Biword indexes store every consecutive word pair as a vocabulary entry for immediate two-word lookup. Positional indexes record exact word offsets within each document, enabling arbitrary multi-word phrase verification and directional or windowed proximity search.

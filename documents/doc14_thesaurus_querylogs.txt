Title: Global Query Expansion Thesauri and Query Log Mining
Author: Susan Dumais
Category: Re-Ranking
Year: 2023
Language: English
ACL: public
---
Global query expansion improves recall by augmenting queries with domain synonyms from a controlled thesaurus, mapping automobile to car and retrieval to search. Search engines also mine historical query logs and clickstreams to recommend co-occurring query terms and apply indirect relevance feedback boosts to frequently clicked documents.

Title: Rocchio Relevance Feedback and Probabilistic Re-Ranking
Author: Joseph Rocchio
Category: Re-Ranking
Year: 2025
Language: English
ACL: public
---
Local relevance feedback refines initial search rankings using explicit or implicit user judgments. The Rocchio algorithm shifts the query vector toward the centroid of relevant documents and away from non-relevant documents. Probabilistic relevance feedback estimates term weights via the Binary Independence Model, while pseudo-relevance feedback automatically expands queries using the top three retrieved results.

Title: Zipfs Law Variable Byte and Elias Gamma Postings Compression
Author: Peter Elias
Category: Compression
Year: 2024
Language: English
ACL: public
---
Zipfs law states that term frequency is inversely proportional to term rank. Frequent words produce small gaps between consecutive document IDs in postings lists. Variable-byte compression encodes gaps using seven data bits and one continuation bit per byte for fast hardware alignment, whereas Elias gamma coding pairs a unary length prefix with a binary offset for bit-level entropy compression.

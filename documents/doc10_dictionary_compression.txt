Title: Heaps Law and Dictionary Compression Techniques
Author: Alistair Moffat
Category: Compression
Year: 2024
Language: English
ACL: public
---
Heaps law predicts vocabulary growth as a power function of collection tokens. Fixed-width dictionary arrays waste substantial RAM because English words average eight characters. Storing the dictionary as a single concatenated string with block pointers every four terms and front-coding shared alphabetical prefixes like automata, automate, automatic, and automation reduces memory footprint dramatically.
